## Brand & Style

### Personality & Emotional Resonance
The design system embodies the role of a steadfast, modern mentor for millions of Indian students navigating scholarships, civil examinations, skill initiatives, and public services. It replaces the anxiety, visual clutter, and opacity of legacy bureaucratic portals with authoritative calm, effortless precision, and civic dignity. The emotional response is one of empowered focus: structured like an institutional civic framework, yet fast, polished, and intuitive like a modern developer productivity suite.

### Design Movement
**Modern Civic Precision (GovTech Meets Linear/Notion Minimalism)**
The interface blends architectural structuralism with pristine utility. It eschews superficial visual effects, neon gradients, and decorative AI clichés in favor of:
- High typographic contrast and disciplined layout grids.
- Explicit visual status architecture (Verified, Approaching Deadline, Action Required).
- Balanced tactile separation with crisp 1px borders, gentle tonal elevation tiers, and intentional information density engineered for scan-intensive mobile and desktop workloads.

## Colors

### Palette Philosophy
The palette establishes instant institutional legitimacy paired with active clarity:
- **Primary Deep Indigo/Navy (`#243B6B`)**: Anchors critical navigation, primary actions, and key active milestones, invoking national prestige, stability, and structure.
- **Deep Slate Navy (`#1A2B4C`)**: Reserved for structural headers, primary banners, and high-impact emphasis layers.
- **Vibrant Accent Teal (`#0F9D8A`) & Light Teal Tint (`#E6F7F5`)**: Directs the student’s focus toward application deadlines, forward progress, verified tags, and calls to action.
- **Neutral Surface Foundations**: Base background is crisp Soft Off-White (`#F7F9FC`), elevating Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) containers with soft structural boundaries (`#E2E8F0`).

### Text & Semantic Roles
- **Headings & Primary Data**: Dark Slate (`#1F2937`) provides WCAG AAA contrast across white and tinted surfaces.
- **Body & Metadata**: Muted Slate (`#64748B`) ensures effortless readability over dense application guidelines.
- **Semantic Status Architecture**:
  - **Success / Verified / Reachable**: Forest Green (`#16A34A`) on Mint Tint (`#DCFCE7`).
  - **Warning / Approaching / Pending Action**: Sunburst Amber (`#D97706`) on Warm Sand Tint (`#FEF3C7`).
  - **Critical / Deadline Passed / Issue**: Crimson Red (`#DC2626`) on Coral Tint (`#FEE2E2`).
  - **Neutral / Informational / Unmonitored**: Slate Zinc (`#64748B`) on Clean Gray Tint (`#F1F5F9`).

## Typography

### Structural Pairing
- **Display & Section Headers (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Delivers geometric warmth with friendly precision. Its balanced apertures make high-density departmental names, examination schemes, and criteria headers readable and non-intimidating.
- **Body & Data Grid (Inter)**: The standard of functional clarity. Optimized for tabular records, dates, procedural guides, and government eligibility documentation with tall x-height and clear numeral differentiation.

### Application Guidance
- Apply strict numerical hierarchy. Large section headings (`headline-xl`, `headline-lg`) must not be crammed next to small controls without at least `1.5rem` structural buffer.
- Numerical statistics (e.g., "₹50,000 / year", "12 Days Remaining") leverage tabular alignment (`font-feature-settings: 'tnum' 1`) in Inter to ensure crisp vertical scanning across cards and tables.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (>= 1200px)**: 12-column responsive fluid grid with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and a max canvas width of `1280px` centered within a `margin` of `2rem` (`32px`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column layout with `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters and `1.5rem` outer margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1rem` outer canvas padding. 

### Spacing Rhythm
Built upon an 8pt layout rhythm with an auxiliary 4pt micro-step (`space-xs` = 4px).
- Internal compact component padding (badges, small inputs, buttons) leverages `space-xs` and `space-sm`.
- Card internal paddings strictly follow `space-lg` (desktop) and `space-md` (mobile).
- Stack groupings between related metadata items use `space-sm` (8px), while distinct modules in scholarship and exam roadmaps rely on `space-xl` (32px) to prevent cognitive overload.

## Elevation & Depth

### Atmospheric Philosophy
Rather than high-blur floating planes or skeuomorphic bevels, the design system employs flat tonal boundaries supported by soft, diffused architectural drop shadows tinted with deep slate navy.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: Background `#F7F9FC` with zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks)**: Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, drop shadow `0 1px 3px rgba(26, 43, 76, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(26, 43, 76, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States, Interactive Cards, Filter Drawers)**: Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, drop shadow `0 4px 12px rgba(26, 43, 76, 0.06), 0 2px 4px rgba(26, 43, 76, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Global Opportunity Drawers, Autocomplete Menus)**: Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, drop shadow `0 12px 28px rgba(26, 43, 76, 0.10), 0 4px 8px rgba(26, 43, 76, 0.04)`.

### Ghost Lines
All elevated components maintain their crisp `1px` boundary even with drop shadows enabled, safeguarding accessibility across low-contrast displays and varying outdoor mobile ambient light conditions.

## Shapes

### Corner Geometry
The system uses deliberate, approachable rounding (`roundedness: 2`, standard radius `0.5rem` / `8px` up to `1rem` / `16px`):
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Dropdowns)**: `0.5rem` (`8px`) to `0.625rem` (`10px`). Crisp, structured, reliable.
- **Container Elements (Cards, Panels, Opportunity Modules)**: `0.75rem` (`12px`) to `1rem` (`16px`). Softens dense documentation without sacrificing layout discipline.
- **Status Pills, Tags, and Badges**: Pill-shaped (`9999px`) or micro-curved (`6px`) to ensure an unmistakable distinction between clickable targets and passive state indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Deep Indigo background (`#243B6B`), pure white label (`#FFFFFF`), `8px` radius, `0.75rem` vertical by `1.25rem` horizontal padding. Hover shifts to `#1A2B4C` with a `0 2px 6px rgba(36, 59, 107, 0.2)` shadow.
- **Secondary Button**: Crisp white background (`#FFFFFF`), `1px solid #CBD5E1`, Dark Slate label (`#1F2937`). Hover introduces `#F1F5F9` background and `#94A3B8` border.
- **Accent Action (Apply / Direct Next)**: Fresh Teal (`#0F9D8A`), white text, hover `#0B8272`. Used selectively for final submission or forward pathway actions.

### Badges & Status Indicators
Always paired with an icon + label for accessibility:
- **Verified / Eligible**: Mint tint background (`#DCFCE7`), deep green text (`#15803D`), leading check icon.
- **Expiring Soon / Urgent**: Sand tint (`#FEF3C7`), dark amber text (`#B45309`), leading clock icon.
- **Action Required / Discrepancy**: Red tint (`#FEE2E2`), crimson text (`#B91C1C`), leading alert icon.
- **Border**: Every badge carries a matching subtle `1px` border (e.g., `#BBF7D0` for verified).

### Opportunity Cards
- Built on `#FFFFFF` with `14px` border radius and `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- Top row: Sponsoring authority / ministry label, verified badge right-aligned.
- Middle: Opportunity name (`headline-sm`, `#1F2937`), eligibility summary snippet (`body-sm`, `#64748B`).
- Footer: Deadline pill, benefit value (e.g., "Full Tuition + Stipend"), and chevron indicator. Hover triggers Level 2 elevation and border transition to `#0F9D8A`.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background: `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, radius `8px`, typography `Inter body-md`.
- Focus state: `1px solid #0F9D8A` with a subtle focus glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(15, 157, 138, 0.15)`). Never standard blue.
- Supporting label in `label-md` (`#1F2937`), helper notes in `body-sm` (`#64748B`).

### Checkbox & Radio Selections
- Box / circle has a `1.5px` border of `#94A3B8`.
- Checked state fills with `#243B6B` and displays an explicit white tick or inner dot.
- High hit-target area (`44px x 44px` touch zone) to accommodate all screen sizes.

### Civic Roadmap Steppers (Domain-Specific)
- A connected linear vertical/horizontal tracking bar indicating required steps (e.g., 1. Aadhar Verification, 2. Income Certificate, 3. Portal Registration, 4. Final Disbursal).
- Completed: `#0F9D8A` solid dot with check. Active: `#243B6B` pulsing ring. Upcoming: Gray circle (`#E2E8F0`) with muted number.
